# Axon Studio - Privacy Policy & Support

**App Name:** Issue Recycle Bin & Restore for Jira
**Vendor:** Axon Studio
**Support Contact:** zhangyinruizhang@gmail.com

## 1. Zero Data Egress (Runs on Atlassian)
Issue Recycle Bin & Restore for Jira is built 100% natively on the Atlassian Forge platform. 
- **No External Servers:** Our app does NOT transmit, send, or store any data outside of Atlassian's cloud infrastructure (Zero Egress).
- **Storage:** All issue snapshots and deleted issue records are stored exclusively within your Jira instance's encrypted Atlassian Forge Key-Value Store (`@forge/kvs`).

## 2. Personal Data
We do not collect, sell, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) on external systems. All data remains under the full control of your organization's Jira administrator.

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion
Administrators can permanently purge any deleted issue record at any time directly from the Issue Recycle Bin admin page. Uninstalling the app removes its access to your Jira instance.
